How this works. Each attempt simulates your trades one at a time. A trade wins with your win rate and pays your ratio times your risk, minus costs, or is drawn from the trade list you pasted. Trailing drawdown is run the way the firm runs it: end-of-day trailing moves the floor only at the close from closed-trade balance and locks at the lock level; intraday trailing follows the highest unrealized point of every trade, so a loser that ran in your favor first still raises the floor; static never moves. Daily limits stop you for the day (soft) or fail you (hard). The attempt passes when it is at the target with the minimum days met and the best single day inside the consistency rule. Funded accounts pay out when the winning days are met, the withdrawable share of profit above the buffer covers the payout, funded consistency passes, and one full stop of room stays above the floor. Costs: monthly fees are charged per month of days used, one-time fees once, resets on every failed attempt where the firm sells them, activation once you pass. Not modeled: news restrictions, inactivity rules, scaling plans beyond contract caps, taxes, tilt. A simulation, not a promise. Not financial advice.
